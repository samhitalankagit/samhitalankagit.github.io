Cost Processor Troubleshooting Agent

Designed an AI experience to help users understand and investigate costing errors on their own, reducing dependence on support teams and service tickets.

01 · THE PROBLEM

The user had the error. The answer lived somewhere else.

When costing processes fail, the system can surface an error without making the underlying cause easy to understand. Users may turn to support teams, knowledge articles or service requests to connect the error with a resolution.

Move the solution closer to the customer instead of moving the customer toward the solution.

02 · PRODUCT GOAL

Help users investigate first, escalate second.

Understand

Explain what the error means in the context of the user's costing process.

Investigate

Surface the most relevant product context and likely causes.

Resolve

Suggest practical checks or corrective actions where evidence supports them.

Escalate with context

If the issue still needs help, give the support path a clearer starting point.

03 · PRODUCT DECISION

Don’t explain the error in isolation.

I designed the experience around the user's real task: understanding what failed, why it failed, and what to do next.

1
Identify the error

Interpret the error and the process context around it.

2
Find relevant knowledge

Retrieve the product guidance and troubleshooting context that matches the issue.

3
Explain and guide

Translate technical information into a concise user-facing explanation and next checks.

04 · BUILD

A 0 → 1 agent from idea to product experience.

I owned the use case, product design, prototype, demo and presentation for an Oracle Agent Hackathon solution. The goal was not to add another search box; it was to make troubleshooting a guided product experience.

04 · DECISION MOMENTS

Guide first. Act only where confidence is high.

Explain vs. search

Bring context into the workflow instead of sending users to another knowledge surface.

Recommend vs. act

Keep the user in control when the system cannot confidently resolve the issue.

Resolve vs. escalate

If the issue still needs support, pass along the investigation context rather than starting from zero.

05 · WHY IT MATTERS
Less dependency on support.

Users can solve more issues without starting from a service request.

Faster path to resolution.

Bring relevant context into the workflow instead of making users search across sources.

AI with a clear job.

Use AI to interpret, connect and guide — not simply answer questions.
