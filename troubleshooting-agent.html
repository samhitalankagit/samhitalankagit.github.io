Cost Processor Troubleshooting Agent

Designed an AI experience to help users investigate costing errors themselves by connecting product context, knowledge retrieval and guided next steps.

01 · THE PROBLEM

The user had the error. The answer lived somewhere else.

When costing processes fail, the system can surface an error without making the underlying cause easy to understand. Users may turn to support teams, knowledge articles or service requests to connect the error with a resolution.

Move the solution closer to the customer instead of moving the customer toward the solution.

02 · MY ROLE

From use case to working product experience.

What I owned

Use case definition, product design, requirements, prototype, demo and presentation for a 0→1 agent solution.

What I brought

Domain knowledge of costing workflows plus hands-on work with LLMs, knowledge bases, prompting and evaluation.

03 · THE AI WORKFLOW

Retrieve context first. Then explain and guide.

04 · PRODUCT DECISIONS

Design the agent around the user's job, not the model.

Explain vs. search

Bring relevant context into the workflow instead of sending users to another knowledge surface.

Recommend vs. act

Keep the user in control when the system cannot confidently resolve the issue.

Resolve vs. escalate

If support is still needed, pass along the investigation context rather than starting from zero.

05 · WHAT I LEARNED
RAG needs a product job.

Retrieval matters when the retrieved context changes the user's next decision.

Prompt evaluation is part of product design.

Relevance, grounding and refusal behaviour matter as much as answer fluency.

Agent UX needs boundaries.

Not every problem should become an autonomous action; confidence and user control shape the experience.
