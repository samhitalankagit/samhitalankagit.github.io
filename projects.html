01 · PRODUCT THESIS

The model is a component. The product is the decision loop around it.

My product work repeatedly moves through the same loop: understand the user decision, identify the signal or context needed, design the interaction, evaluate it, and iterate.

Understand

What is the user trying to accomplish? What context do they already have?

Personalize

What should change based on behavior, intent, history or context?

Evaluate

How do we know the system is useful, correct and behaving as intended?

Act

Can the product help the user complete the next step, not just generate an answer?
