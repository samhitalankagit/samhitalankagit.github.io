A LITTLE ABOUT ME

My product lens started with economics, then grew through AI and enterprise software.

I studied Economics at the University of Hyderabad before starting my career in AI/ML in 2021. Economics trained me to think about incentives, behaviour, trade-offs, demand, supply and how systems respond when one variable changes.

I carry that same lens into product: understand the user and the system, find the important signal, and design the experience around the decision that needs to be made.

I started with machine-learning products, then moved into Oracle and began applying LLMs, RAG, agent workflows and enterprise data to problems in manufacturing and supply chain.

HOW I WORK

Three principles behind my product decisions.

Start with the user's decision.

Understand what the user is trying to decide before deciding what the product should show.

Use AI where it adds leverage.

Keep calculations and business rules deterministic; use AI for investigation, synthesis and explanation.

Make the next action clear.

A useful product should not stop at “here is the answer.” It should help the user know what to do next.
