Real-Time Fraud Detection

Product ownership for a machine-learning platform that detected potential credit-card fraud in real time across ATM, POS and CNP transactions.

01 · THE PROBLEM

The old model detected fraud after the customer experienced it.

The existing approach was reactive: a transaction could be processed, the customer might notice suspicious activity, and only then would the issue be reported and the card blocked.

The product opportunity was to move detection upstream — from post-incident response to transaction-time prevention.

02 · PRODUCT GOAL

Make the fraud decision while the transaction is happening.

Real time

Score transactions within the time window available before authorization completes.

Low friction

Catch genuine fraud without creating unnecessary declines for legitimate customers.

Broad coverage

Support ATM, POS and card-not-present transaction flows.

Actionable decision

Use the score to determine whether the transaction should be allowed or declined.

03 · MY PRODUCT THINKING

The model was only one part of the product.

The key product challenge was connecting machine-learning scoring to a real-time transaction decision without sacrificing latency or creating too much customer friction.

1
Capture transaction signals

Use the available transaction context to identify patterns associated with fraud.

2
Score in real time

Run the ML decisioning path within the transaction-processing window.

3
Act immediately

Decline high-risk transactions rather than waiting for a post-transaction complaint.
