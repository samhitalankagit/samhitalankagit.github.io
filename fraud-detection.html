Real-Time Fraud Detection

Product ownership for a machine-learning platform that detected potential credit-card fraud in real time across ATM, POS and CNP transactions.

01 · THE PROBLEM

The old model detected fraud after the customer experienced it.

The existing approach was reactive: a transaction could be processed, the customer might notice suspicious activity, and only then would the issue be reported and the card blocked.

The product opportunity was to move detection upstream — from post-incident response to transaction-time prevention.

02 · MY ROLE

Product ownership across the decisioning flow.

What I owned

Requirements, solution design, delivery and production implementation for the fraud-detection product.

What I balanced

Detection quality, customer friction, latency and the handoff from a model score to a transaction decision.

04 · PRODUCT DECISIONS

Design around the decision, not the model.

Latency vs. model complexity

A sophisticated model is not useful if the score arrives too late for the transaction flow.

Detection vs. customer friction

False positives can interrupt legitimate customers, so quality must be evaluated in context.

Score vs. action

The product needs to connect the risk signal to an operational decision rather than leave it as an isolated prediction.
