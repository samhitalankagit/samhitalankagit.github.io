AI Agent RAG Structured Data Evaluation Product Design THE PROBLEM Cost investigation is a reasoning task disguised as a data-navigation task. Analysts often need to combine transactions, cost components, manufacturing context and reference documentation to understand why a result changed or a costing process failed. User Cost / manufacturing analyst Trigger Unexpected variance, failed processing or work-order question Goal Understand cause and decide what to check next THE SOLUTION Ground the agent in evidence before asking it to reason. The prototype will combine synthetic structured costing data with a small public-safe knowledge base. The agent retrieves evidence, reasons over it, and presents the answer with clear supporting context. 1 · Retrieve Fetch relevant transactions, cost components and documentation. 2 · Investigate Trace likely drivers and distinguish facts from assumptions. 3 · Explain Return a concise root-cause narrative and recommended checks.
