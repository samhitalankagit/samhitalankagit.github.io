Trace — Evidence-Backed Data Investigation

A small AI investigator exploring whether agents can reduce the distance between noticing a business signal and forming a grounded, testable explanation.

Signal → Evidence → Hypothesis → Next Investigation
SignalWhat changed?

Deterministic analysis identifies the business signal.

EvidenceWhere and when?

Tools investigate segments, countries and time.

HypothesisWhat might explain it?

AI separates supported evidence from speculation.

Investigator Agent

Chooses and runs deterministic analysis tools to gather evidence.

Hypothesis / Critic Agent

Challenges explanations, records uncertainty and identifies missing evidence.

Planner Agent

Decides what investigation should happen next and when the workflow has enough evidence.

Product contract

Code calculates. AI interprets and plans. The user decides.

WHY I'M BUILDING IT

Not just an AI answer generator.

The experiment is deliberately focused on agent reliability: choosing tools, gathering evidence, challenging hypotheses, tracking uncertainty and producing an auditable investigation trail.
