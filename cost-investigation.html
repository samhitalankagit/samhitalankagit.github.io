CostLens

ERP tells you what happened. CostLens explains why.

A product concept for turning manufacturing transactions into an understandable cost story — then helping teams compare alternatives and decide what to do next.

01 · THE GAP

The data is there. The explanation is not.

Manufacturing systems capture work orders, material movements, labor, machine time, WIP and cost transactions. But when a result looks wrong, a user may still have to move across multiple views and piece together the story themselves.

Cost / manufacturing analyst

Needs to explain variances, WIP and product-cost movement quickly, especially around period close.

Plant / operations manager

Needs to know what changed, where the issue is concentrated, and whether action is required.

“Don't just show me the number. Help me understand how I got here.”

02 · THE PRODUCT THESIS

Build an investigation workflow, not a generic chatbot.

The user starts with a business question. CostLens gathers the relevant data, calculates the metric, traces the drivers, explains the evidence, and surfaces the next decision.

1
Explain

Trace a result back to the components, transactions or operational signals behind it.

2
Compare

Put plants, periods, work centers or products side-by-side to expose meaningful differences.

3
Act

Turn the finding into a clear next check or potential action without overstating certainty.

03 · THE PRODUCT SURFACE

Five questions, one underlying investigation model.

Work-order cost

Why is this work order above expected cost? Which component is driving the variance?

WIP intelligence

What is accumulating? Which open orders explain the movement?

Plant comparison

Is it cheaper to run the same product in Plant A or Plant B?

Work-center view

Which operations, machines or scrap patterns are driving the variance?

Product-cost trend

How has unit cost changed over time, and which drivers explain the movement?

Period-end review

What deserves attention before close: unusual variance, aged WIP, or incomplete production?

04 · A PRODUCT DECISION

Let the system calculate. Let AI explain.

Financial and operational metrics should come from explicit business logic. The AI layer is most useful when it connects those metrics, investigates across related evidence, summarizes the story and guides the user.

Deterministic

Variance, contribution, WIP movement, trend and comparison calculations.

AI-assisted

Question interpretation, investigation planning, evidence synthesis and plain-language explanation.

06 · EDGE CASES

Good explanations know what they don't know.

Not enough evidence

Say what is missing instead of inventing a root cause.

Multiple plausible drivers

Show the leading contributors and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

Incomplete work order

Account for timing and remaining production before calling something a true cost problem.

Cheaper ≠ better

A lower modeled cost should still be checked against capacity, scrap, qualification and availability.
