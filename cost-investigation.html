FEATURED PRODUCT · AI · MANUFACTURING

CostLens

ERP tells you what happened. CostLens explains why.

A product concept for helping manufacturing and cost teams understand what happened, why costs or WIP moved, and what needs attention — with an AI investigation layer over deterministic manufacturing analysis.

01 · THE PROBLEM

The data exists. The explanation is the hard part.

Manufacturing systems record work orders, material movements, operations, labor, machine usage, WIP and cost transactions. But when something looks wrong, users often have to move across multiple views to reconstruct what happened.

Cost accountant

Needs to explain variances, WIP and product-cost movement at period end.

Plant / operations manager

Needs a concise explanation of what changed and where attention is needed.

“Don't just give me the number. Help me understand how I got here.”

03 · THE PRODUCT LOOP

Explain → Compare → Act.

The product starts with a business question, gathers the relevant evidence, runs explicit analysis, and then uses AI to synthesize the result into a grounded explanation and next step.

1 · Understand

The agent interprets the user's question and selects the relevant analysis tools.

2 · Calculate

Deterministic logic computes variance, contribution, WIP movement, trends and comparisons.

3 · Explain

The AI separates observed facts, derived metrics and likely explanations.

4 · Act

The output ends with a next check or potential action rather than a generic answer.

06 · PRODUCT GUARDRAILS

Good explanations know what they don't know.

Not enough evidence

Say what is missing instead of inventing a root cause.

Multiple plausible drivers

Show the leading contributors and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

Incomplete work order

Account for timing and remaining production before calling something a true cost problem.

Cheaper ≠ better

A lower modeled cost should still be checked against capacity, scrap, qualification and availability.
