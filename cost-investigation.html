PRODUCT EXPLORATION · MANUFACTURING

CostLens

Manufacturing Cost Intelligence

A product concept for helping manufacturing and cost teams understand what happened, why costs or WIP moved, and what needs attention.

01 · THE PROBLEM

The data exists. The explanation is the hard part.

Manufacturing systems record work orders, material movements, operations, labor, machine usage, WIP and cost transactions. But when something looks wrong, users often have to move across multiple views to reconstruct what happened.

Cost accountant

Needs to explain variances, WIP and product-cost movement at period end.

Plant / operations manager

Needs a concise explanation of what changed and where attention is needed.

“Don't just give me the number. Help me understand how I got here.”

02 · THE QUESTIONS

What should the product help answer?

1
Why is this work order above expected cost?

Trace total variance → cost component → transaction / operation.

2
Why is WIP unusually high?

Connect WIP to open work orders, aging, accumulated cost and production status.

3
How has product cost changed?

Compare periods and identify the largest drivers of movement.

4
What needs attention before period close?

Surface unusual variances, aged WIP and incomplete production.

03 · THE PRODUCT IDEA

Build an investigation workflow, not a generic chatbot.

The experience starts with a business question, gathers the relevant evidence, traces the drivers, and turns the result into a concise explanation and next action.

Calculate

Use deterministic logic for variance, WIP, trend and contribution calculations.

Investigate

Use AI to orchestrate the right analysis steps across connected data and context.

Explain

Separate observed facts, derived metrics and likely explanations.

Act

End with the next check or decision the user can take.

04 · AN EXAMPLE
“Why is WO-10482 18% above expected cost?”
WHAT CHANGEDActual cost is 18% above expected.

Start with the size and shape of the variance.

WHAT DROVE ITMaterial is the largest contributor.

Drill into the components and transactions behind the movement.

WHAT NEXTReview price and quantity movement.

Focus the user's investigation instead of ending at the explanation.

05 · PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
Evidence before explanation.

Every answer should be grounded in the underlying data.

Business logic stays deterministic.

The model should not invent financial calculations.

Useful answers end in action.

The product should help users decide what to check next.

06 · NEXT BUILD

Start small. Prove the investigation loop.

The first version focuses on three workflows: work-order investigation, WIP analysis and product-cost trends. Then expand into a period-end manufacturing review.
