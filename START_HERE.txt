BIDLENS — SIMPLE THREE-SCREEN VERSION

1. Extract this ZIP into a new folder.
2. Double-click Run_BidLens_Windows.bat.
3. Wait for Streamlit to open in your browser.
4. Use the three screens in the left sidebar:
   1 · Refine requirement
   2 · Missing information
   3 · Supplier comparison & chat

The first screen lets you enter free text and get a refined, editable requirement.
The second shows supplier gaps and drafts a clarification email for review/download.
The third shows supplier performance and answers data questions / calculates what-if scenarios.

AI KEY (OPTIONAL)
Open the AI connection expander in the left sidebar and paste your Gemini API key.
The app tries Gemini 2.5 Flash first, with fallback model IDs. If the model API is unavailable,
requirement review falls back to simple rule-based suggestions and the supplier comparison/chat
still uses the local data calculations. Do not paste confidential supplier data into a free API.

IMPORTANT
- All suppliers, prices, and source documents are fictional demo data.
- Emails are drafted for review and download; they are never sent automatically.
- Missing prices/charges remain unknown, not zero.
